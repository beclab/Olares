# Olares Payment

**Olares Payment is the stablecoin payment service of the Olares ecosystem.** You use it to accept USDC / USDT; buyers pay with any wallet like MetaMask; funds arrive **on-chain, straight into your own wallet** — the platform never takes custody.

## Who it's for

- **App developers** — monetize your apps or content
- **Independent merchants and creators** — get paid for your store, services, or digital work
- **Anyone who wants to accept stablecoins** — one LarePass account is all it takes

## Why Olares Payment

| Highlight | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Paid straight to your own wallet** | Buyers transfer directly to your address on-chain. The platform never touches the funds — no pool, no withdrawal step. |
| **One scan to start** | Sign in with LarePass and your merchant account is created on the spot, with receive addresses auto-configured on 7 major chains — your LarePass wallet address. |
| **Zero friction for buyers** | Buyers need no Olares account — MetaMask, OKX, or any other browser wallet works. |
| **Global stablecoin settlement** | USDC / USDT on Optimism, Base, BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum, Arbitrum One, Polygon, and Avalanche. |

## Start in three steps

1. **Sign in** — open [https://www.olares.com/payment/dashboard](https://www.olares.com/payment/dashboard/) and scan with LarePass; your account and receive addresses are ready immediately.
2. **Share or integrate** — generate a fixed-amount invoice link and send it to your buyer to get paid right away, or integrate the API to accept payments in your own site or app (see the developer guide).
3. **Buyers pay, funds arrive** — buyers pick a coin, connect a wallet, and pay on-chain at the checkout; once confirmed, funds land directly in your wallet, visible in the dashboard.
