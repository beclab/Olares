# Save web pages and files in Wise <Badge type="tip" text="Olares 1.12.8+" />

Save web pages to read in Wise, upload files from your device, or download available media and attachments to Olares. Choose the method that fits where you found the content: use LarePass while browsing, paste a link into Wise, or upload a local file.

## Prerequisites

For the full range of ways to save and download content, we recommend installing:

- **Save from your browser**: [Install the LarePass browser extension](../../install-larepass-browser-extension) and connect it to your Olares.
- **Download audio or video**: Search for "YT-DLP" in Market and install it. Not all sites are supported.
- **Save Twitter/X content**: Search for "Twitter" in Market and install the Twitter/X plugin to save posts or download attachments.

## Use the LarePass extension

When you are viewing a page in your desktop browser:

::: tip Save or download
The actions shown depend on the page and the helper services installed on Olares. Saving a page does not automatically download its video or other attached files.
:::

1. Open the LarePass extension.
2. In **Collect**, choose the action you need:
   - **Save to library**: Click the button to add the page to Wise for reading.
   - **Download file**: For supported links:
     1. Choose a video quality or **audio** from the card's menu, if available.
     2. Click the download button and select a folder in **Add task** to save the resource to Olares.

   ![Save or download content in LarePass Collect](/images/manual/olares/wise-larepass-collect1.png#bordered){width=400}

3. Open Wise to find the saved item in **Inbox** or under its content type. For downloads, open the settings menu and select **Downloads** to view the task.

## Add a link in Wise

When you have a URL to a page or resource:

1. In Wise, click <i class="material-symbols-outlined">add_circle</i> and select **Add Link**.
2. Paste the URL. Wise shows the actions available for that link.
3. Choose the action you need:
   - **Save to library**: Click the button to add the page to Wise for reading. Find it in **Inbox** or under the relevant content type.
   - **Download file**: For supported links:
     1. Choose a video quality or **audio** from the card's menu, if available.
     2. Click the download button and select a target folder to save the resource to Olares.
     3. To track the task, open the settings menu and select **Downloads**.

![Available download actions for a link](/images/manual/olares/wise-add-link-download1.png#bordered){width=400}

::: tip Sites that require sign-in

If Wise cannot access a page behind a login, it may need the site's cookies. See [Manage cookies for Wise](./manage-cookies).

:::

## Use Wise on your phone

To add a page or resource from your mobile browser:

1. Copy the page URL from your browser.
2. Open LarePass on your phone and launch Wise.
3. Use **Add Link** to paste the URL, then choose **Save to library** or, when available, **Download file**.

## Upload files in Wise

1. Click <i class="material-symbols-outlined">add_circle</i> and select **Upload**.
2. Select the files to add. Wise supports formats including PDF, EPUB, audio, and video.
3. In the upload window, choose the destination and click **Confirm**.
4. Open the settings menu and select **Uploads** to check progress, then find the uploaded item under its content type.

On desktop, you can also drag files into the Wise window to start an upload.
