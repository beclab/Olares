<!-- #region export-system-logs-steps -->
1. Go to **Settings** > **Advanced** > **Logs**.
2. Click **Collect** to generate a log archive. The archive is automatically saved to the `/Home/pod_logs` directory.
3. Click **Open** to open the logs directory in a new window.

   ![Generate logs](/images/manual/olares/export-log1.png#bordered){width=65%}

4. Right-click the generated archive and select **Download** to save it to your computer.
<!-- #endregion export-system-logs-steps -->
